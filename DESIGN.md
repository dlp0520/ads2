## Brand & Style

This design system expresses celestial romance, divine warmth, and high-end editorial luxury. Designed for an exquisite wedding celebration experience, the visual language balances transcendent spirituality with contemporary refinement. The interface evokes feelings of sacred celebration, timeless intimacy, serenity, and graceful celebration.

The visual style blends **Ethereal Glassmorphism** with **Editorial Classical Luxury**:
- Whispering, semi-translucent alabaster and mist-white panels floating over luminous halo gradients.
- Slender, hair-fine metallic champagne-gold boundaries (`0.75px` to `1px`) evoking artisanal foil stamping.
- Ample negative space that honors every couple's memory and liturgical moment.
- Minimalist micro-ornamentation: four-point celestial stars, restrained stardust particles, and light halos rather than heavy filigree.

## Colors

The color palette translates the radiance of early dawn and celestial light into digital tones:

- **Primary (`#C5A059` - Champagne Gold):** Used for focal calls to action, refined framing, key dates, and monograms. It holds enough warmth to evoke gold leaf without visual glare.
- **Secondary (`#E8D3A2` - Celestial Shimmer):** A soft luminous champagne used for halo backdrops, active tab pills, ambient radial gradients, and decorative divider elements.
- **Tertiary (`#8C7355` - Deep Antique Bronze):** Grounding neutral tone for body text, subtitles, and detailed narrative copy, ensuring WCAG AA contrast over light grounds while avoiding the harshness of pitch black.
- **Neutral Surface Hierarchy:**
  - Base Canvas (`#FDFCFA` - Warm Alabaster): A rich, breathing, warm white background.
  - Card & Container Fill (`#FFFFFF` with 70–85% opacity - Soft Mist White): Ethereal floating surfaces.
  - Border Contrast (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.28)` - Delicate Gold Border): Delicate, gilded hairline strokes.
  - Halo Accent Gradients: Radial blends bridging `rgba(232, 211, 162, 0.35)` down to transparent `rgba(253, 252, 250, 0)`.

## Typography

The typographical pairing expresses ceremonial grandeur alongside contemporary readability:

- **Headlines & Quotes (Playfair Display):** Conveys the weight of timeless vows, editorial invitations, and poetic interludes. Key titles utilize italic variants for emotional inflection (e.g., names of the couple, liturgical verses, poetic quotes).
- **Body, UI, & Metadata (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Provides an open, light, and approachable counterweight. Its soft curves echo the gentle, rounded warmth of the overall art direction while maintaining legibility for itineraries, dress codes, and registry information.
- **Labels & Micro-headers:** Styled in uppercase tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.12em` to `0.14em`) to establish an haute-couture print aesthetic for section anchors (e.g., *ITINERARY*, *DRESS CODE*, *RSVP*).

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial architecture is light and unhurried:

- **Grid Strategy:** A centered single-to-twelve column system with a maximum content container of `1120px` to maintain an intimate editorial reading width. Complex information (such as day-of schedules or gift registries) uses 2 or 3 symmetric floating cards per row.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Generous breathing room between narrative milestones. Sections transition via gentle vertical padding (`space-xl` to `5rem`), preventing information overload and creating an impression of ritualized progression.
- **Responsive Adaptations:**
  - *Desktop (>=1024px):* Generous `3rem` margins, dual-column narrative sections, and horizontal timeline progression for the itinerary.
  - *Tablet (768px - 1023px):* `2rem` margins, cards collapse into 2 columns.
  - *Mobile (<768px):* Single-column flow with `1.25rem` margin, timeline translates into a vertical central thread, cards span full width with cushioned interior padding (`space-md`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through celestial illumination rather than dark, heavy shadows:

- **Halo Glows:** Floating elements project a faint, broad amber-gold luminescence (`box-shadow: 0 16px 40px -12px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.16)`).
- **Frosted Veil Layering:** Secondary cards and sheets utilize multi-layered backdrop blurs (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px); background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.78)`), allowing underlying radial golden auras to subtly filter through.
- **Hairline Gilding:** Every raised card is bounded by a delicate gold border (`1px solid rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.3)`), providing crisp separation against the warm alabaster background without harsh drop-shadows.
- **Z-Index Layering:** Ambient glowing radial orbs sit at base depth (`z-0`), card containers at elevation (`z-10`), sticky navigation bars and interactive modals with increased glass density at upper layers (`z-40` and `z-50`).

## Shapes

The shape hierarchy combines classical architectural arches with gentle modern radii:

- **Cards and Containers:** Rounded corners (`0.75rem` to `1rem`) simulate hand-cut deckle-edged cotton paper.
- **Feature Modules & Image Frames:** Key photographic portraits and invitation highlights incorporate gentle top-arched silhouettes (`border-radius: 120px 120px 16px 16px` on desktop; scaled proportionally on mobile), honoring classical sanctuary architecture.
- **Interactive Controls:** Form elements, buttons, and status tags employ smooth pill shapes or soft rounded bounds (`roundedness: 2`), ensuring soft, tactile touchpoints throughout.

## Components

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Action (RSVP Confirmation, Add to Calendar):** Pill-shaped with subtle champagne gold gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #D4AF67 0%, #B89249 100%)`), crisp white text, and a soft golden ambient glow on hover.
- **Secondary Action (View Registry, Directions):** Transparent background with a `1px` champagne border (`#C5A059`), dark bronze text, and a transition to subtle golden wash (`rgba(232, 211, 162, 0.15)`) upon hover.

### RSVP Form & Form Elements
- **Input Fields & Selectors:** Soft mist translucent backgrounds (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`), hairline gold border (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.4)`), and an elevated warm gold halo ring when focused (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(232, 211, 162, 0.35)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Circular radio rings and soft-square checkboxes framed in gold; selected states display a centered solid golden sparkle or dot.
- **Dietary & Guest Counter Chips:** Pill-shaped tags with light beige tint (`#F5EFEB`), switching to champagne fill with white type upon selection.

### Itinerary Timeline
- **Timeline Structure:** A delicate vertical hairline in muted gold (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.3)`), punctuated by glowing 4-point celestial star icons at each event milestone (Ceremony, Cocktail, Banquet, Celestial Dance).
- **Event Cards:** Floating glass cards staggered along the spine with event time highlighted in tracked uppercase gold labels.

### Gift Registry & Dress Code Cards
- **Registry Item Cards:** Minimalist square cards featuring ethereal imagery, soft gold divider rules, direct external brand links, or seamless monetary gift envelopes with discreet copy-to-clipboard account details.
- **Dress Code Swatches:** Circular color-palette swatches framed in thin gold bezels displaying divine-themed wedding tones (e.g., *Celestial Champagne, Desert Rose, Muted Sage, Warm Alabaster*) accompanied by descriptive editorial attire guidelines.
